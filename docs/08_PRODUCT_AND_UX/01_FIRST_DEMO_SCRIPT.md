# FIRST DEMO SCRIPT (5 MINUTES)

**Status:** Product Aid  
**Date:** 2026-07-20  

Goal: show the Butler clearly, calmly, and convincingly.

---

## Setup
- Dryer selected
- Seed knowledge loaded
- Smith household available optional

---

## Script

1. **Open the app**  
   “This is the Butler. It’s a diagnostic assistant, not a chatbot that guesses.”

2. **Select the dryer**  
   “We’ll use a common problem: no heat, and the drum still turns.”

3. **Start session**  
   Show the first observation question.

4. **Answer 2–3 plain questions**  
   Drum turns normally, then lint filter / vent.

5. **Show the system narrowing the problem**  
   “It’s not jumping straight to replace a part. It’s gathering evidence.”

6. **Show safe guidance**  
   Exterior airflow / lint pathway recommendation.

7. **Verification**  
   “Did that improve drying?”

8. **Memory**  
   “Now the house remembers this root cause and the prevention advice.”

9. **Close**  
   “That’s the point: safer diagnosis, less guesswork, and the household gets smarter over time.”

---

## What the Demo Must Prove

- Observation-first
- No unsafe leap
- Clear reasoning
- Useful ending
- Memory matters

---

*If this demo works, the product story works.*
