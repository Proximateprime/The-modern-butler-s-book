## What is the Butler?

A local-first engineering reasoning assistant for home repair and maintenance.

It is not a generic chatbot.
It uses structured knowledge, evidence, confidence, and safety rules to diagnose problems, while AI mainly handles communication and explanation.

---

## Why does it exist?

To reduce frustration, prevent bad guesses, help people fix things safely, keep good appliances out of landfills, and help homeowners become more capable over time.

One line:
> Help people solve real problems safely — and leave them more capable after every repair.

---

## What must never change?

- Observe before concluding
- Safety gates are hard
- Do not bluff
- Verify before learning
- Access before monetization for essential help
- Local-first / deterministic diagnosis before cloud AI
- Speak human during repair; record engineering in memory
- The core goal is trustworthiness, not maximum AI flash
