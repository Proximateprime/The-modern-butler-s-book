# FAILED REPAIR & ENGINEERING INCIDENT POLICY

**Status:** Locked Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  

---

## Failed Repair Philosophy

Every repair session must end in one of three states:

- **Success**
- **Unresolved**
- **Unexpected Outcome**

Unexpected Outcome is not automatically the user’s fault or the Butler’s fault.  
It is treated as engineering evidence until investigated.

Examples of Unexpected Outcome:
- New symptoms appear after following guidance
- A component fails in an unexpected way
- Smoke, electrical damage, or other adverse results
- The exact recommended steps were followed but produced unexpected results

---

## Engineering Incident Policy

One verified unexpected engineering incident is enough to trigger engineering review.

Do not wait for multiple users if the evidence clearly indicates an unexpected outcome.

When this occurs, the affected Knowledge Package status becomes:

**Engineering Review Required**

until investigation determines the cause, which may include:
- User deviation
- Model variation
- Pre-existing damage
- Package defect
- Environmental condition

---

## Failed Repair Lifecycle

Unexpected Outcome should follow this engineering workflow:

1. Incident Created  
2. Evidence Collection  
3. Engineering Review  
4. Knowledge Package Evaluation  
5. Regression Tests  
6. Updated Package (if needed)  
7. Release  

Every verified engineering incident should permanently improve the system.
