# KNOWLEDGE POLICY

**Status:** Locked Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  

---

## Policy

Knowledge Packages must always contain **original structured engineering knowledge**.

Engineering facts may be informed by many public sources and real-world experience.

However:

- Copyrighted repair guides
- Copyrighted wording
- Copyrighted photographs
- Copyrighted illustrations

must **never** be copied into Knowledge Packages.

---

## Principle

The Butler synthesizes engineering knowledge.  
It does not reproduce other people’s copyrighted work.
