# THE MODERN BUTLER CONSTITUTION

**Status:** Locked Supreme Principles  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  
**Authority:** Highest  

This short document is the constitution of the project.  
No feature, engine, package, prompt, marketing claim, or business decision may violate these rules.

---

## Foundational Rules

1. **Safety over convenience**  
   When safety and convenience conflict, safety wins.

2. **Deterministic reasoning before AI reasoning**  
   Diagnostic logic, confidence, question selection, and safety decisions must be deterministic and testable without an LLM.

3. **Never fabricate evidence or knowledge**  
   The system must not invent observations, failure modes, or certainty.

4. **Never encourage dangerous actions**  
   Hard safety gates are absolute. No skill level, mode, or user request unlocks them.

5. **Engineering truth over marketing**  
   Claims about capability must stay within what the system can actually do.

6. **Access before monetization**  
   Core diagnostic capability and safety guidance must not be locked behind payment.

7. **User dignity at all times**  
   The system never shames, scolds, or talks down to the user.

8. **Knowledge must be verifiable**  
   Production knowledge is subject to review, regression, and the Verification Framework.

9. **Real-world outcomes improve the system**  
   Verified results and Unexpected Outcomes are first-class engineering signals.

10. **Observation before diagnosis**  
    Users describe what they observe. The system performs interpretation.

---

## Conflict Resolution Order

When principles conflict, resolve in this order:

1. Safety  
2. Long-term trust  
3. Engineering truth  
4. User dignity  
5. Access / fairness  
6. Convenience / speed / revenue

---

## Application

- Every major design decision should be checkable against this constitution.
- Cursor and all developers are bound by it.
- Exceptions require explicit founder-level decision and written justification.
