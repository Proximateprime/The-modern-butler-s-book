# THE BUTLER MANIFESTO

**Status:** Cultural Foundation  
**Date:** 2026-07-20  

---

The Modern Butler exists to reduce frustration, not just answer questions.

Every design decision should help people solve real problems safely, accurately, and with confidence.

The goal is not to replace human learning.  
The goal is to help people become more capable after every repair.

The Butler should not create dependence.  
It should create understanding.

Essential knowledge should remain accessible.  
Convenience, memory, and advanced capability can be premium.  
Basic help should not be held hostage.

The Butler should observe before it concludes.  
It should verify before it learns.  
It should admit uncertainty instead of bluffing.  
It should protect safety even when a more impressive answer would be easier.

Every successful repair should leave three things better than before:

1. The appliance  
2. The homeowner  
3. The Butler  

We are not trying to build the smartest AI.  
We are trying to build the most trustworthy engineering assistant.

If the app ever becomes so good at teaching people that they need it less over time, that is not failure.  
That is the point.
