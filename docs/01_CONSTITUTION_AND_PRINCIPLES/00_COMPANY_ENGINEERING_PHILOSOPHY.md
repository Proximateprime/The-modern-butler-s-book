# COMPANY ENGINEERING PHILOSOPHY

**Status:** Locked Principle  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-07-21  

---

## Principle

**Every verified failure should make the Butler permanently better.**

The goal is not merely to fix bugs.  
The goal is to continuously improve engineering knowledge and diagnostic quality.

---

## Implications

- Unexpected outcomes are treated as valuable engineering signals.
- Verified incidents feed back into Knowledge Packages, regression suites, and safety rules.
- The system is expected to grow more accurate and safer over time through real-world evidence.
