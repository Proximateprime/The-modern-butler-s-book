Purpose
This document defines the authoritative lifecycle of every troubleshooting and repair session in The Modern Butler’s Book.
The Butler’s purpose is not limited to restoring function. It exists to help users understand why failures occur and how to reduce the likelihood of recurrence. Every completed session must attempt to identify root cause and generate prevention recommendations. The system improves over the lifetime of a household by remembering prior root causes, maintenance history, and verified outcomes while strictly respecting privacy principles.
This specification governs the Repair Session data model, Evidence Engine, Reasoning Engine, Risk & Safety Engine, Conversation Engine, Household Memory Engine, Learning Engine, and all future implementation work.

Core Principles
The diagnostic workflow is governed by the following non-negotiable principles:

Observation before conclusion
Evidence before assumption
Safety before convenience
Verification before learning
Explainability before fluency
Root Cause before Session Completion
Prevention before Goodbye
No skipping of required phases

The workflow must also support long-term household improvement by retaining and reusing root cause information and maintenance history, and by recognizing maintenance opportunities that arise during repair.

The Diagnostic State Machine
The Butler operates as a finite state machine. All diagnostic activity occurs within one of the defined states. Transitions are strictly controlled.
State Definitions
1. Idle
Purpose: Session has not yet begun.
Responsible Engine: Conversation Engine
Entry Conditions: User opens the app or starts a new troubleshooting flow.
Exit Conditions: User selects an appliance.
Failure Recovery: Return to Idle on app restart or session timeout.
2. Appliance Selection
Purpose: Identify the specific appliance and surface relevant household history, including prior root causes and maintenance records.
Responsible Engine: Conversation Engine + Household Memory Engine
Entry Conditions: User initiates troubleshooting.
Exit Conditions: Valid appliance selected and confirmed.
Failure Recovery: Allow re-selection or creation of new appliance profile.
3. Basic Condition Verification
Purpose: Confirm the appliance is in a valid operating state before diagnosing internal failures.
Responsible Engine: Reasoning Engine + Risk & Safety Engine
Entry Conditions: Appliance selected.
Exit Conditions: All applicable basic conditions verified or explicitly ruled out.
Failure Recovery: If critical conditions cannot be verified, offer safe next steps or recommend professional assistance.
4. Collect Initial Observations
Purpose: Capture the user’s raw observations.
Responsible Engine: Conversation Engine
Entry Conditions: Basic conditions verified or ruled out.
Exit Conditions: Sufficient initial observations recorded.
Failure Recovery: Allow additional observations at any time.
5. Convert Observations into Structured Evidence
Purpose: Transform raw observations into typed Evidence objects.
Responsible Engine: Evidence Engine
Entry Conditions: Initial observations collected.
Exit Conditions: Observations converted into Evidence.
Failure Recovery: Allow re-interpretation or additional evidence entry.
6. Generate Candidate Failure Modes
Purpose: Retrieve possible failure modes from the Knowledge Graph. Candidate generation may be informed by relevant prior root cause patterns stored in Household Memory when they are applicable to the current evidence.
Responsible Engine: Reasoning Engine + Knowledge Graph + Household Memory Engine
Entry Conditions: Structured evidence exists.
Exit Conditions: Ranked list of candidate failure modes generated.
Failure Recovery: Re-evaluate and expand the candidate set when new evidence or historical patterns are introduced.
7. Risk Evaluation
Purpose: Assess safety and risk level of continuing investigation or issuing guidance.
Responsible Engine: Risk & Safety Engine
Entry Conditions: Candidate failure modes exist.
Exit Conditions: Risk assessment complete and safety gates applied.
Failure Recovery: Escalate to professional recommendation if risk is high and confidence is low.
8. Adaptive Question Selection & Evidence Collection (Investigation Loop)
Purpose: Select the highest-value next question or observation request. Question selection may be guided by prior root cause patterns when relevant.
Responsible Engine: Reasoning Engine + Conversation Engine + Household Memory Engine
Entry Conditions: Risk evaluation passed.
Exit Conditions: New evidence collected or decision made to stop investigation.
Failure Recovery: Allow user to provide additional observations.
9. Hypothesis Building & Confidence Update
Purpose: Update and rank hypotheses based on accumulated evidence and relevant historical root cause data.
Responsible Engine: Reasoning Engine
Entry Conditions: New evidence received.
Exit Conditions: Confidence scores recalculated and hypotheses re-ranked.
Failure Recovery: Re-evaluate previous hypotheses when contradictory evidence appears.
10. Decision Point: Sufficient Confidence?
Purpose: Determine whether investigation should continue or guidance may be issued.
Responsible Engine: Reasoning Engine + Risk & Safety Engine
Entry Conditions: After confidence update.
Exit Conditions: Decision to continue investigation or move to guidance.
Failure Recovery: Default to continued investigation on uncertainty.
11. Generate Safe Guidance
Purpose: Produce safe, skill-appropriate next steps. When the appliance is already partially disassembled, the system may identify maintenance opportunities that can be performed with minimal additional effort. Recommendations must clearly distinguish between Required Repair, Optional Maintenance, and Future Convenience Maintenance, and must explain benefits without using fear-based language.
Responsible Engine: Reasoning Engine + Risk & Safety Engine + Conversation Engine
Entry Conditions: Sufficient confidence and risk evaluation passed.
Exit Conditions: Guidance generated and presented.
Failure Recovery: Withhold guidance and recommend professional help if risk is unacceptable.
12. User Performs Action + Verification
Purpose: Record the action taken and verify the outcome.
Responsible Engine: Evidence Engine + Conversation Engine
Entry Conditions: Guidance issued.
Exit Conditions: Outcome verified or recorded as unresolved.
Failure Recovery: Allow multiple verification attempts.
13. Resolution Check
Purpose: Determine whether the reported problem has been resolved.
Responsible Engine: Reasoning Engine
Entry Conditions: Verification evidence exists.
Exit Conditions: Resolution status determined.
Failure Recovery: Return to Investigation Loop if unresolved.
14. Root Cause Analysis
Purpose: Determine why the failure occurred. The Butler must distinguish between Immediate Cause, Root Cause, Contributing Factors, and Preventive Actions. It must reference any prior root cause records for the same appliance stored in Household Memory when relevant.
Responsible Engine: Root Cause & Prevention Engine + Reasoning Engine + Household Memory Engine
Entry Conditions: Problem resolved or investigation complete.
Exit Conditions: Root cause, contributing factors, and immediate cause identified (or noted as undetermined) with supporting evidence.
Failure Recovery: Record when root cause cannot be determined with available evidence.
15. Generate Prevention Recommendations
Purpose: Produce specific, evidence-based prevention recommendations. Recommendations must be derived from root cause analysis, current evidence, and the appliance’s maintenance history stored in Household Memory.
Responsible Engine: Root Cause & Prevention Engine + Household Memory Engine
Entry Conditions: Root cause analysis completed.
Exit Conditions: Prevention recommendations generated and recorded.
Failure Recovery: Provide general prevention guidance when specific root cause is unknown.
16. Create Session Summary & Update Household Memory
Purpose: Produce a permanent record of the session, including root cause, prevention recommendations, and any maintenance performed. Update Household Memory with new root cause data and maintenance actions.
Responsible Engine: Household Memory Engine
Entry Conditions: Prevention recommendations complete.
Exit Conditions: Session summary created and stored.
Failure Recovery: Allow manual review and correction of summary before finalization.
17. Submit Community Learning Candidate (Optional)
Purpose: Propose anonymized, verified learning updates.
Responsible Engine: Learning Engine
Entry Conditions: Session summary complete and outcome verified.
Exit Conditions: Learning candidate submitted or declined.
Failure Recovery: Never submit unverified data.
18. Session Closed
Purpose: Final state. Session is complete and archived.
Responsible Engine: All engines (final handoff)
Entry Conditions: All prior states completed.
Exit Conditions: Session archived.
Failure Recovery: Allow reopening only for correction of verified errors.

Responsibilities of Each Engine
Conversation Engine
Responsible for user interaction and structured presentation of questions and guidance.
Reasoning Engine
Responsible for hypothesis generation, confidence scoring, and interpretation of evidence against the Knowledge Graph and relevant household history (including prior root causes). Does not own truth.
Knowledge Graph
Owns structured engineering truth, including causal relationships between symptoms and failure modes.
Evidence Engine
Responsible for structuring and managing observations as Evidence.
Risk & Safety Engine
Has final authority over safety. May interrupt any state.
Household Memory Engine
Responsible for storing and retrieving appliance-specific history, including past root causes and maintenance actions (vent cleaning, filter cleaning, descaling, coil cleaning, lubrication, inspections, belt replacement, etc.). Provides historical context to improve future diagnostics and prevention recommendations.
Learning Engine
Responsible for proposing updates based solely on verified outcomes. Supports long-term improvement of diagnostics, root cause recognition, prevention recommendations, and maintenance planning across the household’s lifetime while respecting privacy principles.
Root Cause & Prevention Engine
Responsible for distinguishing Immediate Cause from Root Cause, identifying Contributing Factors, and generating prevention recommendations. May reference Household Maintenance History and prior root cause records.
State Machine Controller
Orchestrates state transitions and enforces required phases.

Root Cause Determination
Every completed session must distinguish between:

Immediate Cause — What directly failed or stopped working.
Root Cause — The underlying reason the immediate cause occurred.
Contributing Factors — Conditions that made the failure more likely.
Preventive Actions — Specific steps that can reduce recurrence.

Root cause analysis must consider both current evidence and relevant historical root cause data stored in Household Memory. The system must never present activation of a protective device as the complete explanation when an underlying cause is probable.

Prevention
Every completed session must generate prevention recommendations. Recommendations must be specific, evidence-based, and tailored to the appliance and the household’s maintenance history when available. The Butler’s purpose includes reducing the likelihood of future failures.

Opportunistic Maintenance
When an appliance is already partially disassembled, the system may identify maintenance actions that can be performed at the same time with minimal additional effort. These must be clearly distinguished as Required Repair, Optional Maintenance, or Future Convenience Maintenance. Recommendations must explain benefits without using fear-based language.

Explainability
At every major decision point the system must be able to answer why a question was asked, why a hypothesis was ranked, why confidence changed, why investigation continued or stopped, and why a particular root cause or prevention recommendation was generated.

Failure Recovery
The state machine must handle offline operation, interrupted sessions, contradictory evidence, and new evidence that invalidates previous hypotheses while preserving existing evidence and allowing safe resumption.

Learning
Learning updates may only be proposed after verified outcomes. Every verified repair strengthens future diagnostics, root cause recognition, prevention recommendations, and maintenance planning while respecting the project’s privacy principles. Unverified outcomes never enter Community Intelligence or overwrite core engineering knowledge.

Future Architecture Considerations
Long-term architecture may include appliance-level metadata regarding repairability, reliability, parts availability, and maintenance burden to support repair-versus-replace decisions. Such capabilities are explicitly post-MVP and must never replace current diagnostic reasoning or the requirement to perform root cause analysis.

Safety
This workflow is subordinate to the Safety Invariants and Non-Negotiables. No state transition or guidance may bypass Safety Gates under any circumstances, including Expert Mode.
