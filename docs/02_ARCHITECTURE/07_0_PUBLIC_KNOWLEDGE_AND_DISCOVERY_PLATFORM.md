## Purpose

This module defines how The Modern Butler’s Book shares knowledge with the public.

It is **not** merely an SEO strategy or content marketing plan.  
It is the public-facing extension of the Butler Reasoning Platform.

Its purpose is to:

- Help people before they ever install the app
- Preserve engineering knowledge
- Preserve valuable community knowledge
- Guide users through reasoning instead of dumping answers
- Build long-term trust
- Continuously improve through verified repairs
- Make safe repair knowledge widely accessible

This platform is one of the philosophical pillars of the overall system.

---

## Mission

Many people searching for repair information are already having a stressful day.

The Public Knowledge Platform exists to reduce that stress rather than increase friction.

The goal is not to maximize subscriptions.  
The goal is to maximize successful, safe repairs.

---

## Core Philosophy

The platform must never become:

- Another generic repair wiki
- Another listicle or “Top 10 Reasons” site
- Another AI chatbot that guesses
- A content farm optimized only for search rankings

Instead, it should become:

**A living reasoning encyclopedia.**

Every page should teach.  
Every page should explain.  
Every page should help users understand **why**.

---

## Knowledge vs Information vs Reasoning vs Understanding

| Concept | Meaning in this system |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Information** | Raw facts or statements |
| **Knowledge** | Structured, reliable facts with context and provenance |
| **Reasoning** | The process of using knowledge and evidence to reach conclusions |
| **Understanding** | The user’s ability to see *why* something is true and what it means for their situation |

The Butler focuses on **understanding**.  
Simply presenting answers is insufficient. The system should help people develop clearer mental models of how their appliances work and fail.

---

## Public Diagnostic Pages

The platform should support (among others):

- Model-specific pages
- Symptom pages
- Maintenance pages
- Error code pages
- Failure mode pages
- Safety guidance pages

Whenever appropriate, these pages should be interactive rather than static.

---

## Guided Reasoning Pages

Instead of dumping a list of possible solutions, public pages should guide users through observation-based reasoning:

1. User describes or selects observations
2. The page adapts and narrows possibilities
3. It explains why certain questions are being asked
4. It recommends only safe next steps
5. It clearly indicates when professional help is appropriate

The same core reasoning capabilities that power the app should power these public experiences.

---

## Living Engineering Encyclopedia

Over time the public platform becomes an evolving engineering reference.

Knowledge sources include:

- Manufacturer documentation
- Service manuals
- Verified repair outcomes
- Engineering principles
- Expert contributors
- Community observations (with appropriate trust weighting)
- Patterns learned from Household Memory (anonymized and aggregated)

Every piece of knowledge should carry trust metadata and provenance.

---

## Community Knowledge

Community knowledge is valuable and should be preserved.

However:

- Community observations are **never** automatically treated as engineering truth
- Verified patterns may gradually increase in confidence
- Engineering knowledge remains authoritative
- Provenance and verification status must remain visible

The system should capture useful real-world practices (including those learned by experienced technicians) even when they are not present in official manufacturer documentation.

---

## Search & Discovery

The platform should be discoverable for:

- Specific appliance models
- Specific symptoms
- Specific failure modes
- Maintenance schedules
- Error codes
- Common and long-tail natural language searches

The goal is not SEO tricks.  
The goal is to be the most helpful and trustworthy answer available.

---

## Interactive Public Experience

A visitor should be able to:

- Search
- Make observations
- Learn
- Narrow possibilities
- Understand risks
- Perform safe checks
- Decide whether they need the full app

No login should be required for basic help.

---

## Relationship to the Butler App

| Public Platform | Personal Butler App |
|-----------------|---------------------|
| Free, accessible doorway | Personalized long-term assistant |
| Guided reasoning for common problems | Full diagnostic sessions |
| Educational and safety-focused | Household Memory |
| No account required for basic use | Voice interaction, history, scheduling |
| Builds trust and discovery | Advanced features and convenience |

The free public platform and the personal app are complementary, not competitors.

## Knowledge Feedback Loop

```
Repair Session (App)
        ↓
Evidence + Verification
        ↓
Root Cause
        ↓
Knowledge Graph update (versioned)
        ↓
Public Explanation improved
        ↓
Better discovery via search
        ↓
New users find help
        ↓
More verified repairs
        ↓
Improved knowledge
```

This creates a self-improving knowledge ecosystem.

---

## Public Trust Requirements

The public platform must maintain:

- Transparency
- Explainability
- Clear safety boundaries
- Visible confidence and evidence
- Version history where appropriate
- No clickbait
- No misleading repair claims

Trust is a core product feature.

---

## Public Mission

Helping people comes before maximizing friction.

Core educational content, safety guidance, and basic diagnostic reasoning should remain broadly accessible whenever practical.

---

## New Official Design Principle

**Access Before Monetization**

The Modern Butler’s Book exists to help people solve real-world problems safely and confidently.

Core diagnostic guidance, safety information, maintenance education, and public reasoning resources should remain freely accessible whenever practical.

Monetization should come from added convenience, personalization, professional capabilities, enterprise tools, and advanced features — not from withholding essential repair knowledge.

Every monetization decision should answer one question:

> “Does this sustain the mission, or does it create unnecessary friction for someone who simply needs help?”

This principle is now part of the permanent Product Bible.

---

## Future Vision

The long-term aspiration is for this platform to become one of the world’s most useful explainable repair knowledge bases — not because it has the most pages, but because every page teaches reasoning and helps people understand their problems more clearly.
